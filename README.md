# 👋 Hi, I'm Sayed Zahur Zaidi

**Applied AI & AI Systems Engineer.** I love ML, and I mess with blockchain on the side. 🧠

I'm the founding AI engineer at **[HealthBay](https://healthbay.ai)**, where I work on the agent layer of an AI platform for US healthcare claims. Before this, I founded a startup, took it to paying customers, and closed it when the market said so, which taught me to care as much about distribution and reliability as about the model itself.

> I think about AI systems the way a founder thinks about a product. The hard part is never the model, it's everything from distribution to holding it up in production.

These days the other half of my attention goes to **AI safety**, chain-of-thought faithfulness, activation monitoring, and oversight. I'd rather help build systems we can actually inspect than ones that just score well.
